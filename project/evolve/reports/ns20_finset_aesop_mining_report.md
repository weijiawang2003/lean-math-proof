# NS20 — Final Finset/aesop mining pass

**Branch:** `ns20-finset-aesop-mining`
**Parent:** NS19 (`1910ebf`)
**Goal:** grow the Finset/aesop wrapper-only pool from 4 unique
wins (after NS18 + NS19) to ≥5, closing the NS20 training gate,
by mining the 74 remaining unused Finset theorems in the catalog.
**Outcome:** **0 new wins across 74 thms.** Pool stays at 4
unique. NS21 training NOT justified.

## 7. NS21 verdict

**Training NOT justified.** The Finset/aesop pool is stuck at
4 unique. The hypothesis from NS19 — that 1–2 more wins would
come from re-mining the unused 74 Finset thms — did **not**
materialize: 0 new wins.

Root-cause hypothesis: the 4 Finset/aesop wins from NS18+NS19
share a specific shape — they are coercion / structural
rewrites on `Finset.{coe, cons, disjUnion}` that bare aesop
handles in one step. The remaining unsolved Finset thms
(44 out of 74 mined) involve more substantive combinatorial
reasoning (`Finset.image_*`, `Finset.filter_*`,
`Finset.subset_*` with non-trivial side conditions) that aesop
either times out on or doesn't have the right lemmas to close.

Recommended next moves (NOT NS21 training):

1. **Catalog extension.** The current 200-Finset catalog is
   demonstrably exhausted relative to bare-aesop's reach.
   Pulling fresh Finset surface from Mathlib (especially
   `Finset.image`/`Finset.filter`/`Finset.map` families) would
   either find new aesop-solvable theorems OR confirm that
   bare aesop has hit its capability ceiling. This requires a
   Mathlib introspection script, deferred to a future arc.

2. **Probe richer aesop configurations.** `aesop` without
   options is the weakest configuration. A targeted arc could
   try `aesop (rule_sets [Finset])`,
   `aesop (config := { maxRuleApplications := 200 })`, or
   `aesop` with explicit simp/rewrite lemma bundles. This is
   a wrapper-only probe (no training), similar in shape to
   NS18.

3. **Different family for training.** Move the training arc
   to the `simp_all`/Nat side instead, where catalog extension
   is the bottleneck rather than tactic capability. NS18+NS19
   produced 2 simp_all wins (Nat.mul_mod_mod, Nat.mod_mul_mod);
   a Mathlib-extended Nat catalog could plausibly reach the
   5-gate for that family before Finset/aesop does.

4. **Accept the negative result and stop the
   wrapper-expansion arc.** AlphaEvolve has converged on this
   surface: the routed base model + NS9 wrapper saturates what
   the current catalog + 8-step search can close.
